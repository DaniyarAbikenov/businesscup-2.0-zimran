# UX notes: what confused a first-time user

Walkthrough as a stranger (register, onboarding, Home, start a scenario, compare, decide, document, Progress), desktop 1280 and mobile 375.

## Before
- Home was a stack of 7 panels (privacy banner, follow-ups, 90-day card, describe, create-with-AI, full catalog, cases). Nothing said what the product is for or what to click first.
- Jargon: "situation", "storyline", "engine"-like words, "ledger" tables with no explanation of how to read them.
- Nav had 6 items (Home, Progress, Documents, Journal, Account links, Sign out) and overflowed on mobile.
- A case page showed a results table first; the answer was buried in the details. No sense of where you are in the process.
- Long help paragraphs under fields; no explanation of why we ask for confidence, weights or free hours.

## After
- One promise sentence on Home and onboarding; a skippable 4-step welcome with a one-click worked example (result in about 2 seconds, made-up numbers).
- Home: describe box and four recommended scenario cards above the fold; everything else lower or collapsed. Demo users get a "Start here" strip.
- One word everywhere: "scenario" (was "situation"); "storyline" is "example".
- Navigation: Home, My cases (Journal and Documents as tabs), Progress, Account; "How it works" in header (desktop) and footer. No horizontal scroll at 375 px on Home, case, guide, progress, journal, documents, new scenario, account.
- Every case has a 4-step stepper, a "Best by the numbers" line and a 2-sentence "how to read this" above the results.
- "Why we ask" expanders on confidence, free hours, committed hours, interruptions, weights, estimates and overhead.

## Still not great
- The results tables are dense, especially the estimate and decision ones on a phone.
- The welcome is remembered per browser, not per account.
- Onboarding still shows the main nav before the profile exists.
- Text-only emails; no user testing with real newcomers yet. The next step is to watch 3-5 people use the example without help.
